KanjiVG attribution and license
================================

The kanji stroke graphics used by this deck originate from KanjiVG.

KanjiVG is copyright Ulrich Apel and is released under the Creative Commons
Attribution-Share Alike 3.0 license (CC BY-SA 3.0):

https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/3.0/

KanjiVG project site and documentation:

https://kanjivg.tagaini.net/

This deck converts KanjiVG SVG stroke paths into the generated stroke and
median data used by its Anki writing cards. The original KanjiVG SVG source
collection is not redistributed as a separate source folder in this repository.
The generated media and converted data remain subject to the applicable
KanjiVG attribution and share-alike terms.
