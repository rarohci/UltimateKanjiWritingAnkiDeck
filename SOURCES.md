## KANJIDIC

This deck uses KANJIDIC-derived kanji information obtained through Jisho.
Credit: James William (Jim) Breen and the Electronic Dictionary Research and
Development Group (EDRDG).

- [KANJIDIC project documentation](https://www.edrdg.org/wiki/KANJIDIC_Project.html)
- [EDRDG dictionary license statement and copyright notices](https://www.edrdg.org/edrdg/licence.html)
- [Creative Commons Attribution-ShareAlike 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/4.0/)

The current EDRDG statement applies CC BY-SA 4.0 to the covered dictionary data
and specifies attribution and additional KANJIDIC notices. This material is
included as note fields; it is not covered by this repository's original-code
MIT license. The original downloaded dataset version and any text edits have
not been fully documented.

KANJIDIC describes individual kanji. This credit should not be treated as
confirmation of the source of all vocabulary words or their definitions.
